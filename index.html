Machines that learn. People that imagine.

The Robots, Learning, and Creativity Lab explores how robots and intelligent systems can learn, collaborate, and participate in creative human activities.

Meet the labExplore research
About the lab

Research at the Intersection of human-robot interaction, machine learning, and the performing arts

We study how robots learn from and with people, how creativity can be supported by technology, and how intelligent systems can become meaningful participants in shared activities.

Based atVrije Universiteit Amsterdam
Principal InvestigatorKim Baraka
CommunityResearchers, PhD students, collaborators, and alumni
Research areas

Three connected ways of asking better questions.

01 / ROBOTS

Robots as collaborators

Investigating how robots can interact with people in responsive, social, and meaningful ways.

02 / LEARNING

Learning through interaction

Studying how intelligent systems learn from feedback, environments, and human relationships.

03 / CREATIVITY

Technology for creativity

Exploring new forms of creative practice that emerge when humans and machines work together.
